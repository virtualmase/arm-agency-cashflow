# ARM Agency Pre-Launch Execution Program

**Operating window:** Now through the restoration of the primary domain after August 24  
**Objective:** Convert the domain freeze into a preparation sprint that improves the product, the delivery system, the sales process, the evidence base, and the customer experience before demand is scaled.

## The Practical Answer

The custom-domain freeze prevents a primary-domain launch. It does **not** prevent ARM Agency from improving the work that determines whether demand turns into durable revenue. The current application can still be used on the working deployment for internal demos, owner operations, customer portal testing, checkout validation, and controlled invite-only conversations. The highest-return work now is not more product breadth. It is making the existing five-stream portfolio easy to understand, easy to scope, safe to deliver, and easy to operate after purchase.

> **Operating principle:** Build the delivery and proof system before scaling the acquisition system.

| Workstream | What we can complete before the domain returns | Immediate commercial value | Definition of done |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Launch readiness | Domain, canonical, checkout, webhook, analytics, support, and rollback verification | Makes August 24 an activation event rather than a scramble | A single runbook is executed and signed off before promotion begins. |

| Priority | Asset to build | Why it comes first | Practical output |

| P2 | Marketing distribution system | Distribution should follow proof and delivery readiness | Founder posts, partner outreach, newsletter cadence, and a controlled referral ask after verified client value. |

## Product Delivery: Make the Offers Real

Each sellable offer needs five layers: what the buyer receives, what ARM needs from the buyer, what is explicitly out of scope, how acceptance is determined, and what the next commercial step may be. This protects buyers, protects the delivery team, and creates a credible sales narrative.

| Offer | Client-facing promise boundary | Internal delivery cadence | Natural upsell only after delivery evidence exists |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI Infrastructure Audit | A documented view of the current state, constraints, prioritized risks, and next actions | Intake → evidence review → working session → report → executive readout | Mandate Chain Design Workshop or a tightly scoped implementation plan. |
| Mandate Chain Design Workshop | A facilitated decision-rights and operating-constraint design session | Pre-work → half-day workshop → decision record → follow-up | ARM Core or a custom implementation statement of work. |
| Custom MCP Tool | A defined integration or tool outcome with explicit interfaces and test criteria | Requirements → prototype → test → handoff | Support, managed infrastructure, or additional scoped tools. |
| GEO Retainer | A contracted publishing, entity, and reporting cadence—not a ranking promise | Baseline → approved plan → recurring execution → report | Expanded authority/content scope only after the buyer validates the service cadence. |
| Academy | Learning materials and stated access/support terms | Enrollment → onboarding → curriculum cadence → feedback | Certification, advanced cohort, or implementation support when appropriate. |

## Sales System: From Inquiry to a Safe Yes or No

The contact form should not be treated as a sales process. It is the beginning of one. A consistent next-step process can be prepared now even while the public launch is paused.

| Stage | Owner action | Required artifact | Exit rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New inquiry | Acknowledge and review fit within one business day | Lead record and initial context | Sufficient information to qualify or request clarification. |
| Qualification | Test urgency, buyer authority, problem clarity, budget range, timeline, and delivery fit | Qualification scorecard | Advance only when a specific commercial path is defensible. |
| Discovery | Map current workflow, risk, stakeholders, constraints, and desired decision | Discovery notes and decision map | Produce a bounded next step, not a vague “custom solution.” |
| Proposal | State scope, assumptions, exclusions, price, cadence, and acceptance criteria | Proposal/SOW | Buyer can approve, decline, or request a defined change. |
| Handoff | Confirm owners, access, kickoff, billing, and success cadence | Client onboarding brief | Delivery has a named owner and a first milestone. |

## Content and Marketing We Can Build Without a Domain Launch

Content should support one of two jobs: help a qualified buyer make a decision, or give the team a reusable piece of evidence for a sales conversation. Avoid volume publishing. The first set should be deeply aligned to the five streams and should never rely on customer counts, rankings, uptime, or business outcomes that cannot be documented.

| Stream | Authority asset | Conversion asset | Distribution motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Swell GEO | “What an entity and AI-search readiness audit actually examines” | GEO Retainer scope and deliverables page | Founder post, newsletter, and direct sharing after relevant discovery calls. |
| ARM Mandate | “Decision rights for production AI systems” | AI Infrastructure Audit deliverables and sample agenda | Technical operator outreach and discovery follow-up. |
| Arctura | “When a network membership is useful—and when it is not” | Membership eligibility and participation terms | Invite-only partner conversations. |

| Coreweaver | “What a bounded agent-stack setup includes” | Setup checklist and handoff terms | Productized implementation conversations. |

The working deployment can be used for controlled demonstrations, but broad SEO distribution and paid campaigns should remain paused until the primary domain is restored. Draft content, editorial reviews, email sequences, partner lists, and proof assets can proceed now.

## Backend and Process Improvements That Matter

The revenue dashboard, customer portal, and weekly report are in place. The next operational increment is to make every material event lead to a human decision and an accountable handoff. This is more valuable than adding more charts.

| Control | Cadence | Owner question | Required response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead triage | Daily | Is this a qualified need, a nurture candidate, or a clear no? | Assign a status and the next contact action. |
| Payment reconciliation | Daily | Did every completed Stripe event result in the expected purchase, portal, receipt, and delivery handoff? | Resolve discrepancies before delivery begins. |
| Funnel review | Weekly | Which CTA, offer, and stream create qualified intent? | Continue, repair, or stop one distribution/activity. |
| Delivery review | Weekly per active client | Are scope, access, decisions, risks, and next milestone clear? | Send the agreed update and record escalation needs. |
| Team health review | Monthly | Is workload sustainable and are handoffs creating friction? | Change capacity, scope, or process before quality suffers. |
| Customer health review | Monthly | Is the buyer receiving the contracted value and approaching renewal risk? | Record the account health, next action, and expansion/referral eligibility. |

## What Not to Do During the Freeze

Do not spend on paid acquisition against the expired primary domain. Do not expand the product catalog before the three bounded offers have repeatable delivery systems. Do not create fictional testimonials, case studies, customer counts, performance figures, or outcome guarantees. Do not treat event counts as proof of market traction until source quality and qualified-pipeline data are available.

## The Most Valuable Next Build

The next asset should be the **AI Infrastructure Audit Delivery and Sales Kit**. It connects the home-page diagnostic CTA, Stripe purchase, qualification conversation, delivery workflow, client report, and follow-on ARM implementation path. It is the shortest route to a credible, revenue-capable operating motion while the domain is frozen.
