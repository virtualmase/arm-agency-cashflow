# ARM Agency Pipeline Learning Review

**Purpose:** Convert actual commercial evidence into one or two controlled improvements each review cycle. The review exists to improve fit, clarity, and handoff quality—not to pressure buyers or inflate activity metrics.

## 1. Review Cadence and Evidence

Run this review weekly for active pipeline hygiene and monthly for learning trends. Use only actual CRM records, discovery notes, proposal outcomes, client handoff findings, and delivery feedback. Do not infer reasons from silence or create retrospective stories unsupported by evidence.

| Evidence source | What it can answer | What it cannot prove alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First-party funnel dashboard | How many recorded views, CTA clicks, leads, checkout starts, and confirmed payments occurred | Buyer intent, source quality, or causal impact of one page |
| Lead records | Stated need, contact details, stage, owner, and next action | Organization-wide buying readiness without discovery |
| Discovery notes | Decision context, authority, constraints, and fit | A final commercial reason if the buyer later disengages without explanation |
| Proposal record | Proposed scope, timing, price, and acceptance boundary | Why a buyer chose another path without direct evidence |
| Lost reason | The stated or evidenced reason the motion stopped | A universal conclusion about all buyers |
| Delivery handoff/health | Whether commercial scoping matched delivery reality | Top-of-funnel marketing performance by itself |

## 2. Stage Definitions

| Stage | Entry evidence | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New | Inquiry or approved inbound capture is recorded | First response and owner assigned |
| Contacted | A relevant first response is sent | Conversation booked, nurture path selected, or no-contact reason recorded |
| Qualified | Buyer decision, authority, timing, fit, and constraints have enough evidence | Bounded offer or proposal recommendation is agreed |
| Proposal | Written scope is sent after qualification | Accepted, declined, deferred, or superseded with stated reason |
| Won | Payment/contract state and delivery handoff are complete | Kickoff accepted by delivery owner |
| Lost | Commercial motion is ended or paused | Evidence-backed category and learning note recorded |
| Nurture | Need exists but timing/fit/readiness is not active | Specific future review date or archival decision |

## 3. Lost and Deferred Reason Taxonomy

Use the most specific evidence-supported category. “Other” requires a written explanation. “No response” describes contact state, not buyer motive.

| Category | Definition | Learning question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timing | Buyer confirms a real need but not an active decision window | Is the nurture resource and next-review date appropriate? |
| Budget/procurement | Buyer identifies funding, purchasing, or approval constraint | Was the first scope appropriately bounded and commercially clear? |
| Authority | Required sponsor or decision path cannot engage | Did qualification surface authority early enough? |
| Fit | Need is outside a current bounded offer or delivery capability | Should ARM decline, partner, or create a future offer hypothesis? |
| Scope clarity | Buyer cannot understand the deliverable, boundary, or ownership model | Which part of the sales explanation needs revision? |
| Trust/evidence | Buyer needs evidence that is not available or does not find the approach credible | Is the correct response better evidence, a clearer boundary, or a respectful no? |
| Competition/alternative | Buyer selects an alternative or internal path | What decision criterion did the alternative satisfy? |
| Capacity/timing on ARM side | ARM cannot accept the work sustainably | How should capacity, waitlist, or referral handling improve? |
| No response | Contact ends without a stated reason after the defined follow-up sequence | Was the first response relevant and was the follow-up boundary respected? |

## 6. Controlled Experiment Rules

Only one material commercial variable should change in a defined experiment: for example, one CTA, one qualification question, one service-description section, or one follow-up resource. Every experiment has a duration, an owner, a review date, and a quality constraint. Do not run experiments that promise unverified outcomes, conceal exclusions, manipulate vulnerable buyers, or exceed delivery capacity.

## 7. Review Outcome Options

At the end of each review, choose one of four outcomes for each pattern: **continue** because evidence supports the current path; **repair** because a clear operational gap exists; **test** because evidence supports a bounded hypothesis; or **retire** because the asset, motion, or metric creates more confusion than value. Record material decisions in the owner decision log.
