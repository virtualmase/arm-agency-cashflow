# AI Mastery Source Ledger

| ID | Primary source | Approved public use | Public-claim boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AM-01 | [NIST: AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) | NIST describes the AI RMF as voluntary guidance intended to help incorporate trustworthiness considerations into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI products, services, and systems. The framework’s functional model includes Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage. | Do not characterize the AI RMF as mandatory, legal advice, a compliance certification, or a guarantee that a system is safe or trustworthy. Note that NIST is revising AI RMF 1.0. |
| AM-02 | [NIST AI RMF Playbook: Govern](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/govern/) | NIST’s playbook describes organization-wide policies, processes, practices, documented roles, risk tolerances, documentation, monitoring, and review as governance considerations. | Present roles, documentation, review, and oversight as operating recommendations tailored to context. Do not say a particular organization meets a regulatory or risk-management standard without a scoped assessment. |
| AM-03 | [OECD.AI: A Socio-technical approach to AI literacy](https://oecd.ai/en/wonk/socio-technical-approach-ai-literacy) | The article frames AI literacy around understanding AI, critical evaluation, responsible use, and human-in-the-loop practices, while emphasizing technical and organizational context. | The page carries an author/opinion disclaimer; use it as a contextual literacy perspective, not as binding OECD/GPAI policy or an authority for compliance claims. |

## Drafting controls

The public guides may cite AM-01 and AM-02 for stated NIST framework and governance concepts. They may cite AM-03 for the contextual literacy framing while naming its opinion/disclaimer status in the internal review record. ARM Agency’s AI Mastery methodology must remain clearly identified as a practical operating approach. No guide may promise mastery, credentials, legal compliance, safety, employment, productivity, implementation success, or business outcomes.
