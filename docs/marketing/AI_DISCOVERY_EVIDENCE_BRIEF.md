# AI Discovery Readiness — Evidence Brief

**Purpose:** Keep ARM Agency’s GEO and AI-discovery work grounded in platform guidance rather than unsupported promises. This brief is the factual-review source for related public content, sales material, and delivery scopes.

## Durable Conclusions

Google’s guidance states that its generative-search features remain rooted in core Search ranking and quality systems. It recommends valuable, non-commodity, people-first content alongside clear technical foundations rather than tactics designed solely to manipulate AI responses.[1]

For Google Search specifically, pages must be indexed and eligible to appear with a snippet before they can be eligible for generative-search features. Eligibility does not guarantee crawling, indexing, or inclusion.[1]

Structured data remains useful as part of an overall SEO strategy because it can support rich-result eligibility, but Google states that it is not required for generative AI search and that there is no special schema markup required for AI visibility. Correct markup also does not guarantee a rich-result appearance.[1] [2]

Google explicitly says that `llms.txt` and similar machine-readable files neither help nor harm Google Search visibility. ARM may maintain those files for other systems where useful, but it must not represent them as a Google-ranking or AI-Overview control.[1]

| Safe ARM service claim | Claim to avoid |
| --- | --- |
| “We assess and improve the public content, technical SEO, entity clarity, and reporting foundations relevant to AI-search discovery.” | “We guarantee LLM citations, rankings, AI Overview visibility, or generative-search traffic.” |
| “We use structured data where it accurately represents visible page content and supports the overall SEO strategy.” | “Special schema is mandatory for AI visibility.” |
| “We maintain machine-readable documents for systems that choose to use them.” | “`llms.txt` will increase Google rankings or AI Overview inclusion.” |
| “We measure signals available through verified tools and first-party data.” | “Third-party tools reveal internal model ranking or citation metrics.” |

## Review Rules

Before publishing any GEO statement, confirm that the claim differentiates between **improving a controllable foundation** and **guaranteeing an external platform result**. Structured data must represent visible page content, and no schema should be used to imply reviews, ratings, affiliations, or results that do not exist.[2]
