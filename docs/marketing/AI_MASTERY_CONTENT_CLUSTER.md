# ARM Agency AI Mastery Authority Cluster

## Purpose and boundary

**AI Mastery** is ARM Agency’s public learning-and-capability cluster for agency operators and accountable AI leaders who need to decide how their team will understand, govern, practice, and own AI-enabled work. It is not a certification of competence, a promise of employment or commercial outcomes, or a substitute for legal, security, privacy, procurement, or domain-specific professional advice.

> **Category position:** AI Mastery helps a responsible operator move from AI interest to a named operating capability: a real workflow, a responsible owner, appropriate human oversight, a bounded practice plan, and a decision about whether education, a diagnostic, or scoped implementation is the next fit.

The cluster does not compete with generic prompt libraries or “become an AI expert” promises. Its blue-ocean position is **operational capability with accountable ownership**: the ability to make and review a specific workflow decision, rather than consuming information without a practice context.

## Public claim rules

The guides may describe named frameworks and their recommendations when cited. ARM Agency’s own methods must be framed as recommendations or operating choices. The guides must not claim that a learner will master AI, become employable, achieve a credential, avoid incidents, meet regulatory obligations, or produce a business outcome. Each page will include a visible decision statement, assumptions, readiness checklist, bounded next step, sources, and related decision links.

## Source priorities

The cluster will prefer primary sources with durable applicability: NIST’s AI Risk Management Framework and Playbook for governance and oversight; OECD or UNESCO material for human-centered AI literacy where directly relevant; and established public guidance on workforce AI literacy or responsible use. Secondary commentary may inform wording but cannot substantiate a factual public claim.

## Review cadence

The content owner should review source currency and service fit quarterly, and revise or pause a guide if the underlying framework, service boundary, or evidence basis changes. First-party guide-to-CTA and qualified-conversation signals should determine whether a third AI Mastery page is warranted; page count alone is not a trigger.
