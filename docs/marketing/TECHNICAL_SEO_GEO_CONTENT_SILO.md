# ARM Agency Technical SEO and GEO Authority Silo

## Purpose and boundary

This document defines the public content architecture for ARM Agency’s technical SEO and generative-engine optimization (GEO) practice. Its objective is to make the firm’s operating approach intelligible to accountable buyers while avoiding claims that ARM controls search rankings, model answers, traffic, citations, or revenue outcomes.

> **Category position:** ARM Agency helps accountable teams build an **AI Discovery Operating System**: the governed combination of truthful public information, technical discoverability, evidence controls, and conversion learning required to make a digital presence easier to understand, review, and improve.

The category is deliberately distinct from generic “AI SEO,” content-volume retainers, unbounded technical SEO, and promises to obtain citations or model mentions. It positions the work as a decision and operating system rather than a speculative visibility guarantee.

## Editorial rules

Each page must answer one decision, name the assumptions and boundaries, include a concrete readiness checklist, and offer an appropriate next step. Source-backed guidance must identify the cited authority in visible content and in a references section. Links should help the reader progress to the next decision rather than simply increase page views.

The public experience must remain evidence-safe. Pages will not publish customer counts, third-party performance claims, invented testimonials, model-citation guarantees, traffic projections, search-ranking promises, or claims that an optional crawler or AI-discovery file itself causes visibility.
