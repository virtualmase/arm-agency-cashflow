# ARM Agency Future-Agent Operating System

This directory is the governed onboarding system for future agents and human operators. It translates the current ARM Agency product, sales, service, marketing, operational, and transition controls into repeatable procedures. It does not grant new authority beyond written owner approval, signed client commitments, configured system permissions, or applicable law.

## Operating document map

| Document | Use when | Primary human authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Product and Offer Operating Guide](PRODUCT_AND_OFFER_OPERATING_GUIDE.md) | Explaining offers, choosing a route, preparing scope, or updating product facts | Agency owner and delivery owner |
| [Sales and Qualification Procedure](SALES_AND_QUALIFICATION_PROCEDURE.md) | Responding to inbound demand, preparing discovery, qualifying fit, or preparing a proposal | Sales owner |
| [Customer Service Procedure](CUSTOMER_SERVICE_PROCEDURE.md) | Triaging a support request, preparing a response, handling escalation, or closing a ticket | Client-success owner |
| [Campaign Marketing Procedure](CAMPAIGN_MARKETING_PROCEDURE.md) | Preparing an evidence-safe campaign, content plan, outreach queue, voice/inbox workflow, or measurement review | Growth owner |
| [Agent Governance and Escalation](AGENT_GOVERNANCE_AND_ESCALATION.md) | Assigning a future agent, authorizing a tool, reviewing an exception, or pausing an unsafe workflow | Agency owner |
| [Owner Transition and Exit Readiness Plan](../strategy/OWNER_TRANSITION_AND_EXIT_READINESS_PLAN.md) | Preparing a multi-year succession, sale, or continuity path | Agency owner with qualified legal, tax, and transaction advisers |

## Use order

Future agents start with the entity card, then the governance document, then the task-specific procedure. If task instructions conflict with a current client agreement, a security boundary, a legal obligation, or this system's approval requirements, stop and escalate rather than improvise.

| First question | Required source of truth |
| --- | --- |
| What business and offer am I supporting? | [ARM Agency Entity Card](../strategy/ARM_AGENCY_ENTITY_CARD.md) and the Product and Offer Operating Guide |
| May I communicate externally or act on the account? | Agent Governance and Escalation plus a named approval record |
| What does the customer or prospect need next? | Sales and Qualification Procedure or Customer Service Procedure |
| May I create, send, publish, call, charge, refund, or change an account? | No, unless the specific procedure confirms owner approval and the underlying system permission exists |
