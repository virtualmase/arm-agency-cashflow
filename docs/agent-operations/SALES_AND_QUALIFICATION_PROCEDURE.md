# Sales and Qualification Procedure

## Purpose

Use this procedure for inbound prospect inquiries and owner-approved sales preparation. It operationalizes the Commercial Operating System; it does not authorize a future agent to make a price concession, guarantee an outcome, issue a contract, or commit delivery capacity.

## Intake and triage

Record the prospect's name, organization, contact channel, stated need, source, use case, and consent/contact context. Deduplicate against existing records, preserve the original source, and never enrich with sensitive personal data merely to increase lead volume. If the inquiry is support from a current customer, route it to customer service rather than sales.

| Signal | Route | Agent output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specific operating decision, sponsor, and time-bound problem | Qualification | Discovery brief and proposed questions |
| Clear need for a documented bounded offer | Bounded-offer review | Fit note, inputs needed, and owner review request |
| Educational or early-stage interest | Nurture | Approved resource recommendation and a non-pressured next step |
| Unclear, misaligned, unsafe, or outcome-guarantee request | Clarify or decline | Respectful clarification or boundary response |
| Existing account, payment, portal, cancellation, or delivery matter | Customer service | Service summary with no commercial promise |

## Qualification procedure

Score problem clarity, decision authority, timing, delivery fit, access/participation, commercial readiness, and risk compatibility using the existing 0–2 scorecard. Attach source notes to every score. A total score guides a human conversation; it never auto-approves a sale.

| Score band | Allowed next step | Required human checkpoint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11–14 | Recommend scoped discovery or the relevant bounded engagement | Sales owner confirms fit, capacity, and response |
| 7–10 | Request missing context or recommend a diagnostic | Sales owner reviews the follow-up draft |
| 0–6 | Nurture or respectfully decline | Use an approved resource; do not pursue pressure tactics |

## First-response preparation

Draft a reply within the published one-business-day standard. A valid draft acknowledges the stated need, asks for the decision/workflow/owner/constraint context needed to qualify fit, explains the next bounded step, and states no unverified business result. It must include a human owner or escalation route.

## Discovery and proposal handoff

Before a discovery call, prepare the question set, captured evidence, risks, and recommended route. After the call, produce a decision memo with the buyer decision, owners, constraints, qualification rationale, proposed next step, and follow-up date. A proposal can be drafted only after the sales owner confirms the scope basis; the delivery owner must accept the handoff before kickoff.

## Prohibited actions

Do not send mass outreach, cold-call, book a meeting without the agreed calendar workflow, alter pricing, create a Checkout Session for another person, represent an offer as guaranteed, sign terms, or use a buyer's data beyond the stated sales purpose. Escalate procurement, legal, privacy, security, finance, and scope exceptions.
