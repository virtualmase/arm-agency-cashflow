# Campaign Marketing Procedure

## Purpose

Use this procedure to turn a verified buyer decision into a campaign brief, public asset, approved outreach preparation, and first-party learning loop. This procedure produces drafts and evidence; it does not authorize publishing, paid spend, prospect calls, email sends, or claims without owner approval.

## Campaign brief procedure

Begin with one buyer decision, one audience/job, one approved offer, one bounded CTA, and one measurement question. Use the campaign-brief template in the `arm-campaign-marketing` skill. Check the public claim registry, source ledgers, current delivery capacity, and existing first-party funnel signals before drafting a message.

| Component | Required standard |
| --- | --- |
| Audience | State the decision-maker, job, relevant constraint, and permissible-contact basis. Do not use vague "anyone interested in AI" targeting. |
| Message | Explain a verified operating problem and a bounded next step. Avoid search, model, revenue, speed, or outcome promises. |
| Asset | Link to one evidence-backed guide, offer, or qualification route with matching visible copy and metadata. |
| CTA | Use a diagnostic, scope discussion, learning product, or approved account-bound purchase route appropriate to the decision. |
| Measurement | Define a first-party event, qualified-conversation criterion, owner, review date, and stop condition. |

## Draft production sequence

The evidence analyst prepares a source and claim ledger. The content planner produces channel-specific drafts: landing-page outline, article brief, social post, email, or voice guide. The outreach coordinator prepares an audience queue only from owner-approved records with preserved consent/status. The marketing operations analyst defines attribution and an inspection cadence. The growth owner approves the final asset and any action that can become public or reach a recipient.

## Voice and inbox scope

Start with an inbound web or inbox assistant for information retrieval, lead triage, and human routing. Identify the assistant and provide a human escalation path. A Vapi-style assistant may call a tool only when that tool has a restricted schema, authenticated server endpoint, trusted static parameters, logging, and a human-controlled action boundary. It must not read broad customer data, create charges, execute account changes, or decide eligibility/terms.

Outbound voice requires a reviewed campaign record, lawful permissible-contact basis, recipient-list provenance, disclosure language, caller identity, opt-out procedure, approved script, start/end window, and owner sign-off. Begin with a small internal or consented test group. Do not launch a CSV-driven campaign because the platform exposes that capability.

## Performance review

Review campaign inputs, delivery quality, CTA behavior, qualified lead context, conversion events, opt-outs, and support impact. Treat attribution as evidence, not proof of causality. If data quality, capacity, consent, claim safety, or support coverage is weak, pause or repair the campaign before expanding it.
