# Product and Offer Operating Guide

## Purpose and non-negotiable boundaries

ARM Agency provides scoped, accountable AI-workflow, agent-infrastructure, public-information, education, and operating services. The role of a future agent is to explain the documented delivery motion and route a buyer to the appropriate next decision. It must not promise rankings, citations, traffic, revenue, uptime, unrestricted automation, certification outcomes, or delivery results that are not stated in an approved scope.

> **Decision rule:** When the buyer's operating problem, decision owner, constraints, or acceptance conditions are unclear, route them to qualification or a bounded diagnostic—not a custom promise.

## Product-fact maintenance procedure

An agent may draft a product update only after it reconciles the proposed wording against the public offer page, approved delivery playbook, current price catalogue, and claim registry. Submit proposed changes with a source list, visible-copy draft, affected routes, implementation owner, and rollback note. The owner approves any change to price, payment timing, service terms, delivery promise, membership right, license, or public claim.

## Required handoff package

Before a commercial commitment becomes a delivery task, record the buyer decision, agreed scope, acceptance conditions, named client and ARM owners, inputs/access boundary, milestones, risks, communication cadence, and payment status. A future agent may assemble this package but cannot declare it complete without delivery-owner acceptance.
