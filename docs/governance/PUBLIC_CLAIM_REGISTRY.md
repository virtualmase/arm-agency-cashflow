# Public Claim Registry and Review Protocol

**Purpose:** Keep ARM Agency’s public pages, guides, offers, sales materials, visuals, and metadata accurate as services evolve. This registry prevents operational language from becoming a substitute for evidence.

## Operating Rule

> No material public claim is published, renewed, or expanded without a named owner, a source or delivery-term basis, a review date, and a clear boundary on what the claim does **not** promise.

## Claim Classes

| Class | Permitted basis | Examples | Required control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Service-process claim | Current internal delivery playbook or signed scope | “Engagements begin with named decision owners.” | Playbook owner checks quarterly. |
| Offer-inclusion claim | Current product definition or written statement of work | “A scoped audit produces a findings register.” | Product owner checks before publishing. |
| Technical implementation claim | Tested deployed capability or documented configuration | “Private routes return a noindex header.” | Engineering owner records test/validation date. |
| Factual organizational claim | Verified legal, operational, or public record | “ARM Agency operates five revenue streams.” | Owner confirms source and review date. |
| Educational claim | Credible cited source and context | “Google describes alt text as important image metadata.” | Source is linked and reread when refreshed. |

## Claims Requiring Written Evidence Before Publication

Do not use these categories unless an owner can provide a current source or contract basis.

| Restricted category | Examples to avoid without evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Outcomes and performance | Revenue lift, rankings, citations, traffic, uptime, latency, adoption, quality scores, conversion rates |
| Customer proof | Customer counts, logos, testimonials, reviews, named case studies, retention rates, referrals |
| Availability and support | SLAs, response times, support windows, pilot duration, guarantee language, capacity commitments |
| Technical/security assertions | Certifications, fault tolerance, consensus, privacy mechanism, penetration testing, production status |
| Partnerships and rights | Licensing, network access, revenue share, white-label rights, affiliations, eligibility |

## Review Record

| Field | Required entry |
| --- | --- |
| Claim ID | Stable identifier, e.g. `GEO-OFFER-002` |
| Exact public wording | Copy as it will appear, including visual text if applicable |
| Surface | URL, guide, proposal, sales script, image, metadata, or email |
| Claim class | Select from the permitted classes above |
| Evidence basis | Playbook section, agreement term, test record, source URL, or owner attestation |
| Owner | Person accountable for accuracy |
| Review date | Date verified and next required review |
| Boundary / caveat | What the claim does not guarantee or imply |
| Decision | Approve / revise / remove / defer |

## Review Cadence

| Trigger | Required action |
| --- | --- |
| New public guide, landing-page section, asset, or offer | Review before publication. |
| New price, scope, package, policy, partnership, or support term | Review affected pages and sales assets before announcing. |
| New customer evidence or case study | Obtain written permission and source facts; do not infer outcomes. |
| Quarterly content portfolio review | Sample high-traffic and high-intent pages; retire or revise claims that lack a current owner or basis. |
| Incident, delivery change, or customer escalation | Revisit impacted commitments immediately. |

## Quality Stop Conditions

Publication stops when a claim lacks an owner, cannot be tied to a source, uses a metric without a measurement definition, conflates a plan with delivered work, relies on an external platform outcome, or could reasonably be mistaken for customer proof. The right response is to revise it into a process statement, add the evidence, or remove it.
