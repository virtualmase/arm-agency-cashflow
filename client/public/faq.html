01 / Services

What ARM Agency offers

ARM Agency is the public service platform for Autonomous Resource Management. It provides scoped advisory, implementation, education, and operating services for accountable AI workflows, agent infrastructure, and generative-engine visibility.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is ARM Agency’s ongoing work on entity architecture, structured-data maintenance, research and content operations, and LLM-visibility monitoring. GEO retainers are designed for organizations that want a recurring operating cadence rather than a one-time audit.

02 / Implementation

How work begins

The AI Infrastructure Audit is a one-time bounded assessment of AI infrastructure readiness, operational gaps, decision constraints, and deployment priorities. The published price is USD 2,500.

Use it when: the team needs a prioritized plan before it commits to an implementation program.

The Mandate Chain Design Workshop is a facilitated working session for accountable decision rights, workflow constraints, and implementation scope. The published price is USD 1,500.

03 / Customer access

Purchases, billing, and support

Authenticated customers can use the ARM Agency client portal to view eligible purchases, active subscriptions, and available Stripe invoice links. Subscription cancellation requests are verified against the authenticated customer’s Stripe identity before any change is made.

04 / Trust & scope

Claims, commitments, and outcomes

ARM Agency does not treat a service description, course, audit, or workshop as a guarantee of a business outcome. Scope, success criteria, inclusions, and any contractual commitments must be agreed for the specific engagement.
